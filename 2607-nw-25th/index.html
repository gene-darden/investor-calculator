What this means for you. Sale prices in your zip have stopped falling and are flat to slightly up, which is good news. But sellers are still carrying 2022-era expectations into their list prices, and buyers are punishing it: homes sit for months and then sell well under ask. The homes that move quickly are the ones priced at what the last three closings actually fetched, not at what the neighbor is asking. That is the whole strategy below.
Why $715,000 did not work in 2023, and what has changed

In 2023 the home was marketed at roughly $327 per square foot. Pool homes on freshwater canals in Northwest Cape are closing today between about $230 and $240 per square foot, and even new-construction pool homes are only asking around $290. The 2023 price was about 25% above where the market has settled, which is why it drew looks but not a contract.

Since then you have added real value: a new tile roof, impact windows, shutters and fresh paint. In Florida those items matter more than ever because they directly lower a buyer's insurance quote. That is why I am placing you at the top of the comparable range rather than the middle, at roughly $250 to $260 per square foot.

Low-cost prep that pays for itself
  • Round up the paperwork buyers in Florida ask for first: the 2023 roof permit and warranty, impact-window receipts, and a current wind-mitigation report and 4-point inspection. These can cut a buyer's insurance quote dramatically and are a genuine selling point.
  • Pool and water: have the pool serviced and the heater confirmed working, pressure-wash the deck and seawall cap, and clear anything blocking the canal view from the lanai. The intersecting canal is your single best photo.
  • Curb appeal: fresh mulch, trimmed palms, a clean driveway. The exterior is already painted, so this is a half-day job.
  • Inside: declutter and depersonalize, especially the kitchen counters and primary bath. If you are starting to pack for the move, that works in our favor.
  • Skip: major renovations. At this price point and timeline they will not return the money.
