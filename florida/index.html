The seven-step deal screen

  1. Can it legally rent? County or city certificate eligibility, zoning, HOA covenants, DBPR license path.
  2. What did it actually gross? Trailing-12 owner statements or manager actuals; comps by bedroom count and distance to the beach otherwise, with a haircut.
  3. What does it cost to carry? Property tax at your purchase price, wind and flood quotes, HOA, management at 20 to 25%, utilities, maintenance, capex reserve.
  4. What does the debt look like? Second-home versus DSCR versus cash; jumbo thresholds.
  5. What is the after-tax year one? Cost-seg estimate with 100% bonus depreciation and whether the short-term rental material-participation test is realistic for you.
  6. What is the five-year hold? Appreciation, paydown, exit costs, recapture, 1031 option, IRR.
  7. What is the downside? Occupancy down 15%, ADR down 10%, insurance up 25%, rates up 100 bps. Does it still carry?

Rule of thumb: buy gross rent at 7% or better of price with verified actuals, insure before you inspect, treat the tax engine as a bonus and never the reason, and only buy what you would be happy to own empty for a season.

Year-end matters
